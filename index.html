No Fear Research Papers

Highly technical scientific papers, side-by-side with everyday-English translations — so everyone can understand what's going on.

Why this exists

I built this tool with my Muse AI agent. It takes highly technical scientific papers and gives them side-by-side translations into everyday English, so that everyone can understand what's going on.

My belief is that many great ideas in research papers could be applied to other fields and industry — but the papers themselves are oftentimes unreadable unless you specialize in that specific topic.

Reading some of these papers reminded me of struggling to read Shakespeare in high school. I generally knew what was going on, but the Shakespearean language was hard for me to interpret. (Yes, I relied on No Fear Shakespeare to translate the plays into everyday common language.)

Each translation puts the paper's own words on the left and everyday English on the right. And when the raw data is available, it also includes interactive data visualizations.
